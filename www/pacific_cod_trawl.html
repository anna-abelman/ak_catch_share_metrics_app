PCTC Program

Pacific Cod Trawl Cooperative Program

Implemented

2024

Quota species

BSAI Pacific cod (trawl catcher vessel sector)

Management features

  • Allocation: 77.5% harvester / 22.5% processor quota split.
  • Cooperatives: Required to harvest quota during the A and B seasons.
  • Consolidation: Strict ownership and use caps prevent monopolies.
  • Communities: 12% A-season quota is set aside for Aleutian Islands shoreplants.
  • Bycatch: Halibut limits are reduced by up to 25% and allocated to cooperatives.

Baseline years for catch share metrics

2009–2019 (2004–2009 included for Aleutian Islands endorsements)
